# Categorized Exercise Database

*Note: While many exercises can serve multiple purposes depending on rep ranges and rest times, these are categorized by their primary optimal use cases. The Home category is intended for exercises that do not require specialized gym equipment; equipment-dependent exercises are categorized as Gym unless home equipment access is explicitly supported by the application.*

## Categorization Guide

* **Bodyweight & Plyometrics:** (e.g., Burpees, High Knees, Mountain Climbers) Highly cardiovascular. Best suited for **Losing Weight** (high calorie burn) and **Endurance**, accessible anywhere (Home, Outdoor, Gym).
* **Dumbbell Exercises:** Require dumbbells, which may not be available in every home. In this database, they are categorized as **Gym** exercises so users selecting Home are not recommended equipment-dependent movements by default. If the user later confirms access to dumbbells at home, the system can support equipment-based filtering to make these exercises available.
* **Barbell & Heavy Compound Movements:** (e.g., Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press) Require significant equipment and technique. Categorized higher in difficulty (Intermediate/Advanced). Premier for **Building Muscle**, but also stimulate immense metabolic response for **Losing Weight**. Usually restricted to the Gym.
* **Cable & Machine Exercises:** (e.g., Pushdowns, Flies, Leg Press) Fixed paths make them excellent for Beginners and Intermediates looking to isolate and **Build Muscle**. Exclusively located at the Gym.
